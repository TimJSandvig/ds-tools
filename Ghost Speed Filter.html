Ghost Speed Filter

Pick the speeds you saw during a hunt. Each ghost shows under every speed it can be seen at, with a quick test and a way to rule it out when you have no evidence.

Seen at

Line of sight speeds everything up. Any ghost chasing you in view accelerates from 1.7 toward about 2.8 m/s over roughly 13 s. Judge speed while it is searching.

Blood Moon adds 15% to every ghost, every condition. Shift your reading down before filtering.

Selecting several chips shows ghosts matching any of them. Tap Rule out on a card to grey a ghost out.
